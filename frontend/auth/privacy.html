Local privacy notice

Version: local-pilot-2026-10-15

SAMVED saves your email, a one-way password hash, name, optional city/region, education, academic records, interests, skills, strengths, values, goals, assessments, roadmaps, tasks and progress in the local MySQL database. Registration records when you confirm you are 18+ and accept this notice. Your browser receives a session cookie used to keep you signed in.

If the operator enables AI guidance, bounded education, goal, interest, skill, strength, value and assessment-theme inputs may be sent to NVIDIA. SAMVED excludes your name, email and student ID from those AI requests. Public roadmap catalogue requests may call Parse; external learning and opportunity links open the respective providers.

You can edit your profile, sign out, change your password and permanently delete your account and associated private records from My Profile. Account deletion does not erase external providers' records, database backups or server logs. The local operator controls the database and any backups.

This notice describes the local development app. Career suggestions are exploratory guidance. Before a public deployment, the operator must provide their identity, contact details, retention policy and deployment-specific privacy notice.
